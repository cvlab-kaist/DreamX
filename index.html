Overview

One action space for every embodiment

DreamX turns a pretrained video diffusion model into a latent action learner. Its encoder reads compact latent actions out of human and robot videos while the video prior takes care of appearance, so the latents capture motion alone. The same latent actions then drive video transfer, human-to-robot action transfer, and a compact latent policy.

Method

How DreamX works

One pretrained video diffusion backbone is both the encoder and the decoder.

1

Read out motion

Learnable queries read the video features: 4 tokens per frame, ~300× compression.

2

Reconstruct the future

The decoder rebuilds the future from the first frame + latent actions, so the latents carry motion, not appearance.

3

Reuse anywhere

New first frame → video transfer. Robot state → actions. Compact codes → policy.

(a) Queries → latent actions (yellow) → decoder denoises future video and actions.
